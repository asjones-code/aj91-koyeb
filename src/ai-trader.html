Live experiment  ·  Autonomous spot fund on Kraken

Long/Flat

An AI runs a small, real-money crypto fund. Can it beat just holding Bitcoin?

It trades spot only, so at any moment it is either long a coin or flat in cash. No leverage, no shorting. When markets fall, doing nothing is its only defence, and it is allowed (encouraged, even) to do nothing.

Twice a day it reads hourly market data for nine liquid majors, scores them with a quant framework, and either writes a falsifiable thesis for a trade or explains why it is standing aside. Every number below is live.

The screen

What would make it trade

Momentum, volume and timeframe have to agree before the AI will put money to work. Every 15 minutes a free check (no model call) runs this screen; a coin that lights up all three wakes the AI early.

Trend price above its 4-hour 20-period average · Volume last 24h at or above the 7-day norm · Momentum up on the day. Passing the screen earns a closer look, not a trade.

The skills

A quant framework, not a hunch

The model runs on a written trading skill: a fixed decision procedure it must follow and cite numbers from. If it can't say what would prove a trade wrong, it isn't a trade.

  1. Score momentum

    Each coin gets a 1–10 score from its 24h move, whether volume confirms it, and strength against BTC.

  2. Grade the signal

    HIGH a move over 2% that agrees with the 7-day trend. MEDIUM smaller or mixed. LOW a reversal or noise.

  3. Check timeframes

    Momentum persists for days, then decays. 24h, 7d and 30d must agree; a bounce inside a downtrend doesn't count.

  4. Size by conviction

    LOW ≤ 10% of the fund, MEDIUM ≤ 15%, HIGH ≤ 20%. Never more than a quarter in one coin.

  5. Name the exit

    Every position has a stop sized off volatility, a target at least 1.5× the stop and 3× fees, and a time limit.

The rules

Guardrails it can't argue with

These live in code, not in the prompt. The model proposes; the code checks every order and refuses anything outside the lines.
