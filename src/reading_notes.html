Project · Reading Notes

Years of Kindle highlights,
turned into something you can ask.

Since 2014 I've highlighted the lines that stopped me: philosophy, sailing, startups, attention, Africa and its diaspora, utopias, how to live. This page is a small retrieval-augmented (RAG) system built on those years of reading.

Ask a question and it finds the passages that speak to it, recommends the books they came from, and (when a model is connected) writes a short answer grounded only in those passages.

How it works
  1. Collect. Every highlight and note from my Kindle's My Clippings.txt, parsed, de-duplicated (Kindle re-saves a highlight every time you extend it) and cleaned up.
  2. Retrieve. Your question is matched against every passage with BM25, the classic search ranking. It's fast, free, and runs on the same small server as this site. No vector database, no paid embeddings.
  3. Recommend. Matching passages are grouped by book, so the books that keep answering your question rise to the top.
  4. Generate (optional). The top passages go to a language model with strict instructions: answer only from these excerpts, and cite them. The model is pluggable: a local model or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
