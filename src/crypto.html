Can an AI beat just holding Bitcoin?

An AI model runs a small real-money fund. It trades spot only, so it's either long a coin or in cash: no leverage, no shorting. Twice a day it reads hourly data for nine liquid coins and either writes a falsifiable thesis for a trade or explains why it's waiting.

What would make it trade

Trend, volume and momentum have to agree. A free check runs every 15 minutes, and a coin passing all three wakes the AI early.

Method

A written skill, not a hunch

The model follows a fixed decision procedure and has to cite numbers. If it can't say what would prove a trade wrong, it isn't a trade. The code checks every order against hard limits it can't override.

  1. Score momentum

    A 1–10 score from the 24h move, volume confirmation and strength against BTC.

  2. Grade the signal

    HIGH a move over 2% with the 7-day trend. MEDIUM smaller or mixed. LOW a reversal or noise.

  3. Check timeframes

    24h, 7d and 30d must agree. A bounce inside a downtrend doesn't count.

  4. Read the structure

    Swing highs and lows, volume against price, and false breaks (springs) that get retested on light volume.

  5. Size by conviction

    LOW ≤ 10% of the fund, MEDIUM ≤ 15%, HIGH ≤ 20%.

  6. Name the exit

    A stop beyond a structural level, a target ≥ 1.5× the stop, and a time limit.

The trading terminal (holdings, chart, orders and the AI's controls) is private.

Public figures are percentages only and include trading fees. "Hold seed coins" is what the fund's starting coins would be worth had they never been sold. This is an experiment with a small amount of real money. It can lose it, and none of this is financial advice.
